Wizard's London Hunt

Follow the footprints from Buckingham Palace to the real Diagon Alley street. At every stop: find it, do the mission, try the quest, read the trivia, then tap We found it! to earn house points.

Each stop has three buttons: Directions opens Google Maps, See it now opens Street View so you know what to look for, and Film scene shows pictures of that moment in the movie.
